# Installation

The installer is `OwlTrack-Setup-x.y.z.exe`, where `x.y.z` is the version. The installer has no
wizard. It installs OwlTrack only for your Windows user, so you do not need administrator rights.

## Install OwlTrack

1. Download `OwlTrack-Setup-x.y.z.exe` from
   [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/sefi-uzan/owltrack/releases/latest).
2. Open the file that you downloaded.
3. Wait until the progress bar is complete. The installer then starts OwlTrack.
4. Make your selections on the welcome screen (refer to the next section).
5. Click **Start calibrating**.
6. Do the steps in [Quick start](#/quick-start).

<Note>
The installer puts OwlTrack in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\owltrack`. It also adds OwlTrack to the Start
menu and puts a shortcut on the desktop.
</Note>

## The welcome screen

The welcome screen shows one time, after the first installation.

<Figure src="screenshots/welcome.png" alt="The OwlTrack setup window with two switches and a Start calibrating button" width={560}>
  The welcome screen after the first installation
</Figure>

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Start with Windows** | OwlTrack starts in the system tray when you sign in to Windows. |
| **Desktop shortcut** | Puts an OwlTrack shortcut on the desktop. OwlTrack is always in the Start menu. |

You can change these options at a later time in **Settings → Overlay → App**.

If you install OwlTrack again and your calibration is available, the welcome screen tells you that
OwlTrack kept your settings. Click **Done** to start to use OwlTrack, or click **Open settings**.

</Figure>

## Update OwlTrack

OwlTrack does not look for updates, because it does not connect to the internet.

1. Download the new `OwlTrack-Setup-x.y.z.exe` from
   [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/sefi-uzan/owltrack/releases/latest).
2. Open the file. The installer replaces the old version.

The update keeps your settings and your calibration.

## Uninstall OwlTrack

1. In Windows, open **Settings → Apps → Installed apps**.
2. Find **OwlTrack** in the list.
3. Click **⋯** and then click **Uninstall**.

The uninstaller also removes the **Start with Windows** entry.

OwlTrack keeps your settings and the glyphs that it recorded in `%APPDATA%\OwlTrack`. To remove all
data, delete this folder after you uninstall OwlTrack.

<Caution>
When you delete `%APPDATA%\OwlTrack`, you lose your calibration. You must calibrate OwlTrack again
after the next installation.
</Caution>
