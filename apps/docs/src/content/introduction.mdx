# Introduction

OwlTrack is a small, always-on-top overlay for MapleStory on Windows. It **reads your level, character
name and EXP straight from the game window** and shows:

- **Last 5 min**: EXP gained in the last five minutes
- **EXP/h**: your rate over the last 30 minutes (or the whole session, if it's shorter)
- **This session**: total EXP gained since you started, and for how long
- **To next level**: how long until you level at the current rate

It never touches the game's memory, files or network traffic. It looks at the screen, the same way you
do, using OCR that runs entirely on your PC.

<Callout>
OwlTrack is free and open source under the MIT license. It's a fan-made tool and isn't affiliated with
or endorsed by NEXON.
</Callout>

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- The game running **windowed** or **borderless**. An overlay can't sit on top of exclusive fullscreen.
