# Calibration

The **Calibrate** tab shows a frame from the game on the left and the regions on the right. The badge
next to **Calibrate** in the sidebar counts how many of the three required regions are set.

## Drawing a region

Select a region in the list and drag a box on the frame. Boxes are stored relative to the game
window, so they survive moving the window. If you change the game's resolution or UI scale, redraw
them.

- **Grab frame** takes a fresh picture of the game, e.g. after you moved the camera or closed a menu.
- The zoom slider enlarges the frame so you can draw precisely.
- The bin icon clears a region.

## Thresholds

OCR doesn't read the game's colours directly. Each region is turned into a black-and-white "text
mask", and the threshold is the brightness cut-off between text and background. The preview under a
region is exactly that mask.

- Letters broken up or missing: **lower** the threshold.
- Background showing up as black blobs: **raise** it.

The defaults work for most setups. The name uses a low cut-off on purpose: thin strokes like the
crossbar of an `f` disappear above ~120.

## Fixing the character name

OCR sometimes confuses look-alike letters in the game font (`f`/`t`, `l`/`I`). Fix the name once in
**Overlay → Character name**, or hover the name on the overlay and click the pencil. The fix is stored
for the spelling OCR detected, so it sticks for that character only.

## Meso notices (experimental)

Turn on **Count mesos** (the switch next to **Meso notices**, or in the Overlay tab) and draw a box
around the "You have gained mesos (+52)" lines on the right of the screen.

This is approximate: lines the OCR can't read cleanly are skipped, so it undercounts. EXP always comes
from the status bar, never from notices, and item drops aren't tracked.
